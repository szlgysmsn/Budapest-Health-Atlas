# Original research and impact roadmap

1. **Verify:** Check all 23 reported district values against the 2023 source and record a dated audit log.
2. **Request mentorship:** Seek feedback from a qualified Budapest geography / environmental science / public health researcher before starting fieldwork.
3. **Field study design:** Define safe public street segments across contrasting neighborhoods, comparable observation times, a measurement protocol for shade coverage, repeat samples, and a data dictionary.
4. **Collect:** Personally collect observations in the field, recording dates, conditions, locations and missingness without identifying passersby. Do not invent readings or claim photographs show unmeasured temperatures.
5. **Analyze:** Publish original data, reproducible code, uncertainty, and whether conclusions agree or disagree with district-level vegetation patterns.
6. **Share:** Approach local organizations with a one-page summary; track only real feedback and verified adoption.
