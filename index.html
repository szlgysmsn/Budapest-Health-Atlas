The vegetation–heat relationship

Each point is one district. Toggle the emissions scenario. Values are model-derived, not observed temperatures.

Vegetation cover = published mapped vegetated land cover, not accessible public parkland. Heat metric = number of days per year where the modeled 30-year mean daily maximum reaches 30°C, averaged by district.

The mid-century comparison

Mean modeled days, six least-green versus six greenest districts.

This contrast is descriptive and not causal: vegetation is included in the source climate model. District boundaries, terrain, and urban form also differ.

Model-metric threshold: 30 days

03 / METHODS & LIMITATIONS

Rigorous about what we can say.

The project contributes secondary calculations and a transparent interface, not novel primary data or independent climate simulations.

    What we did not establish
