PRIVACY: Nothing entered here is sent to us or GitHub. Records are saved only in this browser using local storage. Export your CSV regularly or you may lose data if browser storage is cleared. Do not record names, faces, license plates, or private addresses.

How to conduct the pilot

  1. Ask a geography/science teacher to review the sampling and safety plan before collecting observations.
  2. Choose four 100-meter public street segments in District II and four in District VII, documenting a consistent selection rule. Do not choose streets solely for dramatic contrasts.
  3. Observe from a safe public sidewalk during similar daytime and weather conditions. Estimate the fraction of the 100-meter sidewalk in tree shade. Record date, time, clouds, side of street and visible trees.
  4. Enter each real observation below. Export to CSV and review possible bias from time of day, weather, buildings and street selection. Do not make citywide or medical claims from eight segments.
