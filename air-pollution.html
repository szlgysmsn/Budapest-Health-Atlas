Medical meaning and uncertainty

PM2.5 is relevant to cardiovascular and respiratory illness, and NO₂ is associated with traffic-related exposure and respiratory health. However, these mapped estimates cannot establish that one district has a higher disease burden, and area means must not be treated as formal compliance readings.
