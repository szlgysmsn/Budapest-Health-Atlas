# Research methodology

Study type: secondary descriptive district-level environmental comparison of all 23 Budapest districts.

**Underlying sources:** Vegetation-cover fractions reported by Csóka Gergely (2023), based on national NÖSZTÉP land-cover sources dating approximately 2015–2017; heat projections from OMSZ KlimAdat / ALADIN-Climate / SURFEX as presented in the same source.

**Outcome definition:** Number of days per year whose *modeled 30-year mean daily maximum temperature* reaches or exceeds 30°C, averaged within each district. This is **not** observed heat-day frequency and should not be treated as residents' individual exposure.

**Analysis:** Rank districts by published vegetation percentages. Compare mean model heat outputs for the six lowest and six highest vegetation-ranked districts, with equal weights per district, separately for the 2041–2070 RCP4.5 and RCP8.5 scenarios. This is exploratory and descriptive.

**Limitations:** Land cover informs climate modeling, so comparisons do not establish causation. District averages obscure street-level conditions; scenario projection periods differ from vegetation-data years. No population weighting, clinical health measurements, causal adjustment, independent model fitting, field data, external scientific validation, or ethics-reviewed human-subjects survey is included. Schematic tile grids are not GIS geographical maps.
